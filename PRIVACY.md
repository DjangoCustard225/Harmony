# Harmony Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Harmony is an offline music player. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

## On your device

- Your music, library, playlists and settings stay on your device.
- Harmony has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Artist information

When **Fetch artist info** is on in Settings, Harmony sends the name of an artist to [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com/legal/privacy) and, as a fallback, [TheAudioDB](https://www.theaudiodb.com) to load a photo and biography. Nothing else is sent. Turn this off in Settings to keep Harmony fully offline.

## Your files

Harmony reads only the music files and folders you import or link. It does not upload them anywhere.
